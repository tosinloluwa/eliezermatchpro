Our Mission

Inspired by the biblical story of Eliezer's careful search for a wife for Isaac (Genesis 24), our mission is simple: to connect Christian singles with life partners who share their values, beliefs, and vision for a Christ-centered marriage.

At EliezerMatch, we believe that love is more than a feeling—it's a sacred journey, designed by God, to unite two hearts in faith, purpose, and commitment.

What Sets Us Apart

Faith-First Matching

We match you with someone who shares your devotion to Christ and your commitment to a godly marriage.

Beyond Surface Level

Our process looks beyond hobbies and interests to explore your beliefs, values, and vision for life—ensuring a foundation for lasting love.

Prayerful Guidance

Our team of relationship experts and spiritual advisors prayerfully pairs you with someone who complements your journey.

Spiritual Foundation

We strongly depend on prayers and spiritual intuition led by the Holy Spirit for guidance and counseling, both before and after compatibility assessments.
